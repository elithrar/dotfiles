# Physical artwork and contour-cut products

Distinguish printed artwork, layered solid-color vinyl, masks/stencils, and printed contour-cut decals. Establish finished size, substrate, application surface/face, adhesive side, finish, and cut behavior. Preserve the editable master separately from flattened or outlined production output.

Reuse accepted logos, glyphs, ticks, stripes, and other canonical artwork rather than approximating them from memory. Preserve repeated dimensions, radii, cadence, and deliberate layer relationships. Reconstructed graphics require a defensible physical scale and explicit uncertainty.

Keep print artwork/bleed, finished cut contour, special inks, cutting operations, registration, and proof-only content separate. Do not let a proof stroke become a physical cut or an expanded fill produce duplicate contours.

Retrieve the exact product/vendor requirements for bleed, safe area, cutline names, spot colors, line weight, effective raster resolution, color profile, transparency, and file format. Do not treat one printer's values as universal. Extend artwork through required bleed and keep critical marks in the safe area.

Verify closed cut contours, smoothing, minimum islands/notches, and reliable cutting/weeding. For clear, reflective, metallic, or specialty stock, establish white-ink, knockout, substrate, and background intent. Preserve declared colors/profiles without silent conversion; digital color previews are approximate unless physically or instrumentally verified.

Inspect the production export at useful detail and final physical size: expected artwork elements, clipping, missing/extra shapes, overlays, bleed, margins, borders, orientation, and layer order. Compare with the accepted source and confirm physical bounds and canonical geometry across formats. For print-and-cut delivery, inspect the producer's proof interpretation when authorized and available.
