# Standards and evidence boundary

Select the convention required by the project; do not mix incompatible notation or claim compliance from appearance. Retrieve the applicable edition and relevant primary requirements when needed. Public catalogue descriptions establish scope, not access to every normative clause. If only those descriptions are available, say the drawing uses established conventions, not that a full compliance audit passed.

## Australian projects

Start with the asset owner, project specification, drawing purpose and accepted examples. Record the applicable document and revision in the design record. Check the current issue and any contractually specified edition. Use the following public sources to choose an approach.

- **General drafting.** AS 1100.101 covers drawing sheets, lines, lettering, scales, projections and dimensioning. Check the edition and amendments for the project. For mechanical drawings also check the applicable AS 1100.201 requirements. [Standards Australia, AS 1100.101](https://store.standards.org.au/product/as-1100-101-1992)
- **Terminal drawings.** Water Corporation DS24, version 1 revision 3 (March 2026), §§23.2–23.4, describes physical strip views and a tabular form for larger installations. Inspect drawings 23-1 through 23-3 (pp. 189–191) for terminal order, spares, cable/core identification and paired terminations at a junction box. The control example uses one common screen note. Derive the actual screen connection from the project. [DS24, electrical drafting](https://pw-cdn.watercorporation.com.au/-/media/watercorp/documents/about-us/suppliers-and-contractors/resources/design-standards/ds24-00-electrical-drafting.pdf?hash=2B62F44C3649970CF23E7809CC27E6D8&la=en&rev=dfb355f64f5e4f4db436f4544294ac5b#page=189)
- **Lettering, notes and materials.** DS24 §§5.5, 5.10 and 14 give this owner's lettering hierarchy, limits on general notes and material schedule format. Its 2.5 mm lettering for terminal IDs and notes and 3.0 mm column headings are specified at A1. Check the actual delivery size and reductions. Main Roads WA §§7.3, 7.5–7.6 and 10.3–10.5 give another owner's rules for greyscale, legends, positive imperative local notes, terminal identification and BOM references. [DS24 current issue index](https://www.watercorporation.com.au/about-us/suppliers-and-contractors/resources/design-standards) · [Main Roads WA electrical and ITS asset drawing guidelines](https://www.mainroads.wa.gov.au/globalassets/technical-commercial/technical-library/electrical-and-its-standards-engineering/specifications-and-drawing-guidelines/electrical-and-intelligent-transport-systems-infrastructure-asset-drawing-guidelines.pdf#page=10)
- **Legacy references.** DS24 §2.2 identifies AS/NZS 1102.103 (conductors and connecting devices) and AS/NZS 4383.3 (connection diagrams, tables and lists) as withdrawn. An owner may retain that notation for its assets. Cite the owner's applicable instruction when using it. AS/NZS 3000 concerns electrical installation requirements. A claim about wiring-rule compliance requires separate installation evidence. [DS24, §2.2](https://pw-cdn.watercorporation.com.au/-/media/watercorp/documents/about-us/suppliers-and-contractors/resources/design-standards/ds24-00-electrical-drafting.pdf?hash=2B62F44C3649970CF23E7809CC27E6D8&la=en&rev=dfb355f64f5e4f4db436f4544294ac5b#page=21) · [Standards Australia, Wiring Rules](https://www.standards.org.au/flagship-projects/wiring-rules)

## International and US references

- [IEC 61082-1:2014](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/4469): electrotechnical document, diagram, drawing, and table presentation.
- IEC 60617 is the diagram-symbol library. IEC 60417 covers markings used on equipment. Check the specified library for the function and symbol variant. [IEC 60617 database](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/2723) · [IEC TC 3 scope of each library](https://tc3.iec.ch/standard-as-database/)
- IEC 62027 covers presentation of engineering object lists, including parts lists. [IEC 62027:2011](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/6319)
- [ASME Y14.100-2017](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-100-engineering-drawing-practices): engineering drawings and associated lists.
- [ASME Y14.3-2012 (R2024)](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-3-orthographic-pictorial-views) and [Y14.5-2018 (R2024)](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-5-dimensioning-tolerancing): views, dimensioning, and tolerancing.
- [ISO 128-2:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/83355.html): technical line and leader conventions; use the relevant ISO projection/dimensioning standards when the project specifies ISO.

For actual hardware and manufacturing limits, prioritize the exact manufacturer's current drawing/application specification and applicable producer requirements. Keep source provenance available off-sheet. Do not invent firm-specific standards, reproduce protected standard sheets, fabricate approvals, or imply professional certification.
